title: Anécdotas
title_en: Little Jokes
level: A1
author: Anécdotas populares · E. S. Harrison, An Elementary Spanish Reader (1912)
description: Eight very short jokes from an old Spanish schoolbook.

—¿[Ha leído Vd.|Have you read] [Romeo y Julieta|Romeo and Juliet]?

—[Romeo|Romeo], [hace mucho tiempo|a long time ago]; [pero|but] [no he leído|I haven't read] [Julieta|Juliet].

* * *

—¿[Sabe Vd.|Do you know how to] [nadar|swim], [Gómez|Gómez]?

—[Sí|Yes], [mi capitán|captain].

—¿[Dónde|Where] [lo ha aprendido Vd.|did you learn it]?

—[En el agua|In the water], [mi capitán|captain].

* * *

—[Tiene Vd.|You have] [un hermoso paraguas|a beautiful umbrella].

—[Sí|Yes], [es un regalo|it's a present].

—¡[De veras|Really]! [y|and] ¿[de quién|from whom]?

—[No lo sé|I don't know]; [pero|but] [dice|it says] [en el mango|on the handle], «[Presentado a|Presented to] [Juan Pérez|Juan Pérez]».

* * *

[Comía|Was eating] [un inglés|an Englishman] [en una fonda|at an inn] [y|and] [le sacaron|they brought him], [como era natural|naturally], [pan tierno|fresh bread].

—[Tráigame|Bring me] [pan duro|stale bread] —[dijo|he said] [al camarero|to the waiter].

—[No lo hay|There isn't any], [señor|sir] —[contestó|answered] [éste|the waiter].

—[Pues|Then] [que lo hagan|have them make some]. [Yo esperaré|I'll wait].

* * *

—¡[Papá|Dad], [papá|Dad]! [Planté|I planted] [patatas|potatoes] [en el huerto|in the vegetable garden], [y|and] ¿[sabes|do you know] [lo que|what] [ha salido|has come up]?

—[Ya lo creo|Of course]. [Patatas|Potatoes].

—[No|No], [papá|Dad]; [han salido|there came out] [unos puercos|some pigs] [y|and] [se las han comido|they ate them up].

* * *

[Un individuo|A man] [que|who] [venía|was traveling] [a Madrid|to Madrid] [en diligencia|by stagecoach], [entró|went] [en una posada|into an inn] [a las doce del día|at twelve noon] [y|and] [preguntó|asked]:

—¿[Cuánto vale|How much is] [la comida|lunch]?

—[Doce reales|Twelve reales (old coins)].

—¿[Y la cena|And dinner]?

—[Ocho|Eight].

—[Pues|Then] [déme Vd.|give me] [de cenar|dinner].

* * *

—¿[Qué tienes|What's the matter], [José|José]?

—¡[Estoy desesperado|I'm desperate]!

—¿[Por qué|Why]?

—[Se me ha perdido|I've lost] [el perro|my dog].

—¿[Y|And] [por eso|that's why] [te desesperas|you're in despair]?

—¡[Ya lo creo|You bet]! [Y|And] [te juro|I swear to you] [que|that] [si no aparece|if he doesn't turn up], [le mato|I'll kill him].

* * *

[En cierta ocasión|Once] [preguntó|asked] [un comerciante|a merchant] [a un marinero|a sailor]:

—¿[En dónde|Where] [murió|did die] [su padre|your father]?

—[En el mar|At sea].

—¿[Y su abuelo|And your grandfather]?

—[En el mar|At sea].

—¿[Y su bisabuelo|And your great-grandfather]?

—[Señor|Sir], [también|also] [murió|he died] [en el mar|at sea] [como|like] [los otros dos|the other two].

—¡[Ah|Ah], [miserable|you wretch]! —[dijo|said] [el comerciante|the merchant]—. [Después de|After] [esos ejemplos|those examples] ¿[todavía|still] [se atreve Vd.|do you dare] [a embarcarse|to go to sea]?

[Calló|Was silent] [el marinero|the sailor], [meditó|he thought] [algunos momentos|for a few moments] [y|and] [dijo|said] [después|then] [al comerciante|to the merchant]:

—¿[En dónde|Where] [murió|did die] [el padre de Vd.|your father]?

—[En la cama|In bed].

—¿[Y su abuelo|And your grandfather]?

—[En la cama|In bed].

—¿[Y su bisabuelo|And your great-grandfather]?

—[En la cama|In bed] [también|too].

—¡[Ah|Ah], [miserable|you wretch]! —[dijo|said] [entonces|then] [el marinero|the sailor]—. [Después de|After] [esos ejemplos|those examples] ¿[todavía|still] [se atreve Vd.|do you dare] [a acostarse|to go to bed]?
